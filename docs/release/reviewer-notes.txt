Pikolumi does not require an account or login. All 2,400 activities in 16 sections are bundled and accessible immediately. No purchases or subscriptions are offered.

Parent area: tap the lock icon. On a fresh install, answer the adult multiplication question, then choose and confirm your own six-digit PIN. There is no fixed reviewer PIN. The parent session locks when the app goes into the background.

In the parent area, choose the interface/question language and an explorer's learning language separately. For example, choose an English interface with Arabic learning, then open Letters, Word garden or Trace & Write. English, French, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese and Arabic are supported. Sound and reduced-motion settings are also in the parent area.

Coloring and Drawing studio support fills, brushes, undo/redo, zoom, full-screen canvas and reset. Leave a page and reopen it to verify saved artwork. Each avatar profile has separate artwork, favorites and progress. Trace & Write preserves its letter proportions and partial stroke progress when rotating the device.

Activities and essential spoken guidance work in airplane mode immediately after installation. Ads require connectivity; missing or failed ads never prevent access. Banners appear on library screens and are hidden inside activities. Interstitials can appear after eligible completions, with limits shared across profiles and launches: at least five minutes of foreground use, four completed activities, five minutes between interstitials and at most six per rolling hour.

Privacy controls, artwork export and external support links are behind the parent gate. Support and privacy: https://seifelmoulouk.com/pikolumi/en.html and https://seifelmoulouk.com/pikolumi/en.html#privacy. Support email: rahmani@seifelmoulouk.com.
